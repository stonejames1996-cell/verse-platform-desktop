VERSE PLATFORM FOR WINDOWS - QUICK START
========================================

INSTALL
1. Double click "Verse Platform Setup 1.0.1.exe".
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
3. Click through the installer and leave "Launch Verse Platform now" ticked.
4. Verse opens in its own window. Log in like you do on the website.
   Closing the window keeps it running in your system tray (bottom right, click ^ if you can't see it).

HOW IT WORKS
Play  >  it captures  >  save locally  >  preview and trim  >  publish to Verse

- It starts recording by itself when you're in a game. (League: it waits in the lobby
  and kicks in when the match loads.)
- Press ALT + F10 to save the last 60 seconds. You'll hear a ding.
- Clips stay on your PC. Nothing goes online unless you publish it.

POST A CLIP
1. Click the CLIPS tab on the left edge of Verse (or My clips).
2. Open a clip and drag the handles to pick the part to post (up to 60 seconds).
3. Click Publish to Verse. Verse's Upload screen opens with your clip loaded and the game picked.
4. Add your caption and hashtags, then post like normal.

WHERE CLIPS ARE SAVED
C:\Users\<your name>\Videos\Verse Platform\<game name>

TIPS
- Change the shortcut or clip length: My clips, then the gear icon.
- Game not detected? With the game open: Settings, Games, Add a game.
- Play in borderless windowed mode, or clips may come out black.
- Record your mic: Settings, Audio, Microphone.
