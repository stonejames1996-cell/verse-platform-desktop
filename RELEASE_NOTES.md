## What's new in 1.0.3

* **Auto highlights (League of Legends):** your multi kills, aces and Baron or Dragon steals are clipped by themselves, no key needed. A whole fight becomes one clip named after the best moment, like "Triple kill". Turn it off or switch to "Every kill" in Settings, Games.
* **Save pop-up:** "Last 60s saved by Verse Platform" now shows in the corner of your game when a clip saves. It's click-through, never takes focus, and never shows up in your clips. Turn it off in Settings, General.
* Auto clips get an **Auto** tag in My clips.
