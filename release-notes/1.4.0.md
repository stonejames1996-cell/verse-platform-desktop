## What's new in 1.4.0

* **Verse pauses itself while you play.** When a game starts, the app window swaps to a simple "Verse is paused while you play" screen, whatever page you were on (Home, My clips or Settings). Nothing runs in it, so it takes nothing from your game. Recording, highlights and overlays keep working exactly the same.
* **Back where you left off.** When the game closes, Verse goes back to the page you were on, with any new clips already in My clips. Want it open mid game? Click **Open anyway**.
* **Minimising counts too.** Minimising the window now pauses it like closing to the tray does, and it picks up where you were when you bring it back.
