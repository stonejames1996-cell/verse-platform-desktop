## What's new in 1.4.4

* **WoW pop-up out of the way.** In World of Warcraft the "saved" pop-up now shows at the top middle, so it no longer covers your character frame.
* **Move it in WoW.** Press Alt+Shift+M in WoW, drag the pop-up where you like, then press it again to save. WoW keeps its own spot, and there's a Reset spot button in Settings.
* **WoW logo everywhere.** The World of Warcraft settings and your WoW: Forever clips now show the WoW logo, nice and sharp.
* **Fix:** moving overlays in one game no longer resets the spots you picked in another game.
