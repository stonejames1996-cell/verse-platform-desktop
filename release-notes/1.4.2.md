## What's new in 1.4.2

* **No more typing /combatlog.** Verse now adds a tiny Verse Platform add-on to World of Warcraft that turns the combat log on for you every time you log in, so your WoW highlights just work. It does nothing else. Works for WoW: Forever, Classic and retail.
* **First time only:** type **/reload** in WoW or log in again so the add-on loads. You'll see "Verse Platform: combat log on" in chat when it's working.
* **Your choice.** Don't want the add-on? Switch off "Turn on the combat log by itself" in Settings > World of Warcraft and Verse takes it back out.
