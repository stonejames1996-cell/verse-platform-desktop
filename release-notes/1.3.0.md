## What's new in 1.3.0: a big League update

* **Live benchmarks:** a small box on the right shows how your game is going against your own average: CS per minute (with a little line of how it's moved), gold per minute, kill share, K/D/A, and your level vs your lane opponent. Green means above your usual, yellow below.
* **Objective timers:** the same box counts down when Dragon, Elder Dragon, Baron and inhibitors come back, and says when they're up.
* **Your game so far while you're dead:** the item ideas pop-up now also shows your CS, kill share and vision vs your average, and your items' gold vs the enemy in your lane.
* **Match summary:** when a game ends you get a summary card with your K/D/A, CS, kill share, vision and how many highlights were saved.
* **Match analysis in My clips:** open a League game and under its clips you'll find Farming, Combat and Vision tabs, each with a score, the biggest thing that stood out, a chart over the whole game and a few tips.
* **Compare with other players (OP.GG):** the analysis shows your rank, compares your KDA with players at your rank on your champion and your farming with that champion's top players, and adds your real gold, damage and the lobby's average rank a few minutes after the game.
* Every one of these can be switched off in Settings under League of Legends in game. The box on the right never shows in your clips.
