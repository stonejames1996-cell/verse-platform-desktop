* **Counter-Strike 2 and Dota 2 highlights:** auto clips now work in CS2 (2K, 3K, 4K, Ace, Victory) and Dota 2 (First blood, Double Kill up to Rampage, Teamfights, Victory). The app sets it up by itself. Dota 2 also needs `-gamestateintegration` in its Steam launch options.
* **Match folders for CS2 and Dota 2:** clips are grouped by game, like "Premier on Mirage" or "Dota as Shadow Fiend", with your K/D/A and result.
* **Review my deaths:** turn it on in Settings and the app saves the lead-up to every death in a private Deaths section in each match, so you can see what went wrong. Works in League, CS2 and Dota 2.
* **Squad POV (beta):** share a squad code with your duo or stack. When one of you gets a highlight, everyone's app clips that same moment from their own screen, and you get both screens side by side in one video.
* **"Clipped by Verse Platform" is now on every clip.** It shows for the first 2 seconds and can't be turned off.
* Auto clips, death reviews and squad clips now queue up instead of skipping each other when they happen at the same time.
* The app keeps 2 minutes of recording on hand (even with a short clip length) so long plays and squad clips don't get cut off.
