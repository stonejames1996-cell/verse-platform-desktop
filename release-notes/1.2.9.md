## What's new in 1.2.9

* **League item ideas:** when you die in League, a small pop-up suggests counter items based on what the enemy team has, like anti heal against Soraka and Aatrox or Guardian Angel against a crit Kai'Sa.
* It shows the enemy champions' pictures next to each reason, so you can tell who it means at a glance.
* It always gives you a few choices with the reason, and disappears when you respawn. Press Alt+Shift+I in a match to see it any time.
* It uses the same info as the Tab screen. Clip while it's open and it shows in the clip too. Turn it off any time in Settings under League item ideas.
* **Records the screen your game is on:** with more than one screen, Verse now records whichever one the game is open on, even if you move it mid game. Save pop-ups show up there too. You can switch this off in Settings and pick a screen yourself.
