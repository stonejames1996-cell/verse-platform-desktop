* **Works with Discord screen share:** sharing your screen on Discord could leave clips choppy, frozen or silent. On Windows 11 the app now records with Windows' newer capture method, which runs happily alongside Discord, OBS and other screen sharers.
* **Backup plan built in:** if a capture method won't start, keeps freezing or gets held back, the app switches to another one by itself for the rest of the session.
* Sound is now recorded separately from the picture inside the app, so a slow picture can't hold up the sound any more.
