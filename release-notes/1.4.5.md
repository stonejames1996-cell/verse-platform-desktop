## What's new in 1.4.5

* **"Clipped by Verse Platform" card always lands.** If the app was closed or updated before the card was added, it now adds it next time it opens. Clips from the last few days that missed it get it now.
* **Busy clips get their card too.** If a clip was open in a player when the card was being added, the app now tries again a bit later.
* **Smoother WoW recording.** Standing still, reading quests or going AFK in WoW no longer counts as the recording freezing, so it keeps using the best capture method all session.
* **WoW logo on your sessions.** WoW session folders in My clips now show the WoW logo instead of a plain square.
* **Fix:** update checks failing with a "certificate" error on some PCs.
