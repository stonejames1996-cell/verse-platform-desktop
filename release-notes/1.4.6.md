## What's new in 1.4.6

* **Better recording in every game.** Loading screens, menus and going AFK no longer count as the recording freezing. Before, a still loading screen could drop the app to a slower recording method that looked worse and used more CPU for the rest of your session.
* **A fresh start each game.** Every new game starts back on the best recording method, instead of sticking with a slower one picked earlier.
