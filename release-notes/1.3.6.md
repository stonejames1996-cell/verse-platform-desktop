## What's new in 1.3.6

* **New: The Finals auto highlights.** Double, triple and quad kills, Multi-kills (5 or more), team wipes, cashouts and cashout steals now save by themselves. The app reads the game's own pop-ups, so keep your HUD on.
* **New: The Finals in game.** A pop-up on each elimination with its name (like Double kill or Team wipe), a small "your game so far" box with your eliminations, cashouts, steals and cash earned, and a session summary when you close the game. Each session gets its own folder in My clips.
* **New: a Settings section for each game.** League of Legends, The Finals, Counter-Strike 2 and Dota 2 each have their own highlight choices, so turning something off in one game leaves the others alone.
* **Tidier Settings.** Every section starts closed. Click one to open it.
* The "Last 60s saved by Verse Platform" pop-up is always on now and shows up in your clips.
