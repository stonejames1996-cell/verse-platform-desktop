## What's new in 1.4.1

* **World of Warcraft auto highlights.** Verse now clips your best WoW moments by itself: boss kills in dungeons and raids, big pulls (4 or more mobs at once), your biggest hit and biggest heal of the session, and killing blows on enemy players, including Double, Triple, Quadra and Pentakills. Boss wipes and your deaths can be clipped too for a blooper reel (off to start with).
* **Turn it on with /combatlog.** The highlights come from WoW's own combat log, so type **/combatlog** in chat each time you log in. Verse only reads that file, nothing touches the game. If you forget, Verse reminds you.
* **Pick what gets clipped.** There's a new World of Warcraft section in Settings with a switch for each moment. It works for WoW: Forever, Classic and retail.
* **WoW: Forever by name.** Your clips are now labelled WoW: Forever (or WoW Classic) instead of just World of Warcraft, and each play session gets its own folder in My clips.
