## What's new in 1.3.4

* **Move the overlays:** press Alt+Shift+M in game, drag the stats box, the stats and item ideas pop-up, and the save and summary pop-ups wherever you like, then press it again to save. They stay put between games, and Settings has a Reset spots button.
* **Hide the stats box:** press Alt+Shift+B in League to hide the box on the right, and again to bring it back.
* **Match analysis, tidied up:** it's now closed by default, showing just your three scores. Click it to open the full analysis.
* **A better chart:** smooth lines, the game's lane, mid and late phases marked, champion pictures and numbers at the end of each line, and hover anywhere to see everyone's numbers at that minute.
* **Champion pictures:** "Compared with" shows your lane opponent's picture, and the chart's key shows yours and theirs.
* **Clearer lane tips:** when you catch up on your lane opponent but finish just behind, it now says you closed the gap.
* **Tidier stats pop-up:** text no longer spills out of the boxes.
* All three League shortcuts can be changed in Settings.
