## What's new in 1.2.8

* **Your highlight is in the clip now:** when Verse saves a highlight like a Double kill, Pentakill, Ace or Rampage, the clip shows the "Clipped by Verse Platform" tag first, then a matching highlight card right when the moment happens.
* It's part of the video, so it shows on Verse, Discord, TikTok and everywhere else.
* Combos read nicely too, like "Triple kill + Ace".
* Trimmed and vertical copies keep the card as long as the big moment is still in them.
