## What's new in 1.5.0

* **A brand new look.** The app now matches verseplatform.gg: Verse blue, the same fonts as the site and glass pop-ups.
* **See what you're recording.** The top bar shows the game's icon with a red live dot while it records.
* **Cleaner My clips.** Game icons on every filter, tidier stat tiles on your matches and cleaner clip labels.
* **Easier trimming.** The clip viewer shows a strip of frames from your clip behind the trim bar, so you can find the play.
* **Jump straight to your game in Settings.** There's now a list down the left with each game's icon.
* **Fresh paused screen.** The screen you see while playing got the new look and shows your game's icon.
* **Verse blue in game.** The save pop-up and the Clipped by card now use Verse blue. Same size and spot as before.
