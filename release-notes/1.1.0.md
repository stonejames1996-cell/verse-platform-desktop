* **Vertical clips for TikTok, Shorts and Reels:** open a clip and pick **Blurred background** or **Centre crop**. You get a 9:16 copy with the Verse tag, saved in My clips.
* **Weekly recap:** one click in My clips stitches your best clips from the last 7 days into a video up to 60 seconds long, ready to post.
* **Say "clip that"** to save a clip (turn it on in Settings). It uses Windows' own speech recognition, so nothing leaves your PC.
* **Controller buttons:** save a clip with View + RB on an Xbox style controller, or pick another combo in Settings.
* **Remove my voice:** clips recorded with a mic now keep the game sound on its own track, so you can post without your voice.
* **Captions ready to go:** posts from the app come with a caption and hashtags filled in, like "Pentakill on Brand in ARAM 🔥".
* **Star clips** you want to keep, and **Storage** in Settings shows how much space your clips use and can clean up old ones automatically. Starred and posted clips are always kept.
