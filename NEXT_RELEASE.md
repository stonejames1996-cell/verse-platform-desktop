# Next release (not released yet)
Changes waiting for the next version. When releasing, bump APP_VERSION and move these into release-notes/<version>.md.

* **Works with Discord screen share:** sharing your screen on Discord could leave clips choppy, frozen or silent. The app now notices when its recording is being held back and switches to Windows' newer capture method, which runs happily alongside Discord.
* Sound is now recorded separately from the picture inside the app, so a slow picture can't hold up the sound any more.
