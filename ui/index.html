    In game

    Small pop-ups over League, using only what you can already see in game. Press ALT + SHIFT + I in a match to see your stats and item ideas any time. Shows in borderless or windowed mode.

    Stats and item ideas shortcutClick, then press the keys you want. Shows the pop-up any time in a match
    Your stats and item ideas while you're deadCS, kill share and vision vs your own average, your items' gold vs your lane, and counter item ideas. Shows in clips taken while it's open
    Hide or show the stats box shortcutClick, then press the keys you want. Hides the box on the right (benchmarks and timers) until you press it again
    Move the overlays shortcutPress it in League, drag the boxes where you want them, then press it again to save. Works for the stats box, the stats and item ideas pop-up, and the save and summary pop-ups
    Live benchmarksA small box on the right with your CS and gold per minute, kill share and K/D/A vs your own average, and your level vs your lane opponent. Never shows in clips
    Objective timersCounts down Dragon, Baron and inhibitor respawns on the right of the screen. Never shows in clips
    Match summaryA summary card when the game ends. The numbers also show on the game in My clips
    Compare with other players (OP.GG)Shows your rank in the match analysis, compares your KDA with players at your rank on your champion and your farming with that champion's top players, and adds your real gold and damage after the game. Uses your Riot ID and server with OP.GG

    The Finals

    The Finals doesn't share live game data with apps, so highlights come from reading its own pop-ups under the crosshair. Keep the game's HUD on. The overlays only use your own numbers.

    Highlights: eliminations
    Highlights: team and cash
    In game
    Elimination cardsA card on each elimination with its name, like Double kill or Team wipe, in the same spot and style as the Clipped by Verse Platform card. Shows in your clips
    Your game so farA small box with your eliminations, team wipes, cashouts, steals, best multi kill and cash earned. Never shows in clips
    Hide or show the box shortcutClick, then press the keys you want. Hides "your game so far" until you press it again. Can be the same keys as League's
    Move the overlays shortcutPress it in The Finals, drag the box and the elimination pop-up where you want them, then press it again to save
    Session summaryWhen you close the game, your numbers vs your usual. They also show on the session in My clips

    World of Warcraft

    Works with WoW: Forever, Classic and retail. Highlights come from WoW's own combat log. The app only reads that file, nothing touches the game.

    Turn on the combat log by itselfAdds a tiny Verse add-on to WoW that switches the combat log on every time you log in, so you never have to type /combatlog. It does nothing else. The first time, type /reload in WoW or log in again
    Move the save pop-up shortcutPress it in WoW, drag the box where you want the "saved" pop-up, then press it again to save. It starts at the top middle so it stays clear of your character frame
    Highlights: dungeons and questing
    Highlights: PvP

    Auto highlights

    Saves your best plays by itself in League of Legends, The Finals, Counter-Strike 2 and Dota 2. Pick what counts for each game in its own section.

    Auto clip highlightsSaves short clips of your plays by itself, no key needed
    Clip lengthAround the whole play, so a fight is one clip
    Review my deathsSaves the lead up to every death in a private Deaths folder inside the match, so you can see what went wrong. Doesn't count towards the limit below.
    Most auto clips per game

    Squad POV Beta

    Share a squad code with your duo or stack. When one of you gets a highlight, everyone's app clips that exact moment from their own screen, and you get both screens side by side in one video.

    Squad codeMake a code and send it to your mates, or paste theirs
    Your name in the squadBlank uses your in-game name
    Send my POV to my squadA small copy of your view of their highlight goes to them through Verse, so they get the side by side
